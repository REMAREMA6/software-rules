## Cross-facet review requirements

1. **Boundary:** Each case states included and excluded components, external dependencies, and environment assumptions.
2. **Property identity:** Every required property has a stable ID, facet, subject, dimension, and status.
3. **Evidence sufficiency:** Captured/partial properties link to exact observations and retained evidence. Source-inferred rules are not relabelled as runtime observations.
4. **Replica independence:** The replica does not call the source implementation to produce its result. A different language/framework/database alone is not enough if it delegates the behaviour back to the source.
5. **Discrimination:** Probes include normal and relevant boundary/negative/failure cases. Mutations must alter a meaningful rule and be detected.
6. **Comparison:** Missing or unobserved properties remain missing/unknown, never matches. Performance values are only compared under equivalent workload and environment conditions; different implementations may have different measured costs.
7. **N/A discipline:** Not-applicable is valid only relative to a declared system boundary, with a rationale. It cannot erase a facet from the project-wide catalogue.
8. **Completion:** A facet is complete only after every required property in its reviewed inventory is evidenced or explicitly resolved. A case's local completeness does not imply facet-wide completeness.

## Current audit decision

This inventory exposes material gaps in all facets, even where bounded cases already pass. PROM-PERF-001 and CASBIN-ADV-AUTHZ-001 have passed their declared bounded runtime and evidence gates. Their success does not close the remaining property classes listed above. The Phase 6 exit gate remains open. The next audit must convert each unresolved gap into a case, a justified case-boundary exclusion, or an explicit unresolved limitation; it must not silently close gaps to make the tracker green.
